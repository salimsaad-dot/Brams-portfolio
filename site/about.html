My Philosophy of Education

I believe every child can learn when the teacher is patient, prepared and fair. I will use active methods like group work and role-play, and model respect, honesty and discipline. I will use simple local materials to teach well despite large classes and limited resources, and I will keep reflecting on feedback to improve.

Both my Year One and my reviewed statements stress that all pupils can learn, and that teachers must be caring, respectful and prepared.

My reviewed statement is based on real classroom experience. It adds active methods, coping with limited resources and reflection. These changes come from my school attachment and the guidance of my mentor and STS partner.
